# 04 · Sales playbook

## The sales motion

```
Lead ─► Discovery call (15 min) ─► Live demo on THEIR matter (20–30 min) ─► 14-day team trial
    ─► Day-3 check-in ─► Day-10 review call ─► Proposal ─► Paid (annual preferred)
```

Small chambers close in 1–3 weeks. Mid-size firms: 4–10 weeks with a paid pilot.

## 1. Discovery call (15 minutes)

Goal: find the pain, qualify, and get a real question or draft to use in the demo.

1. "Walk me through your practice — what kind of matters, which courts?"
2. "Who does the research and first drafts today? How long does a typical research question take?"
3. "What did you have to change in your templates after BNS and BNSS came in?"
4. "Have you or your juniors tried ChatGPT or similar? What happened?" *(Listen for fake-citation stories — they sell Citation Guard for you.)*
5. "Which subscription databases do you use?" *(Position as complementary.)*
6. "If a tool saved each advocate here five hours a week, what would you do with that time?"
7. "Who besides you would decide on something like this? How do you normally pay for software?"
8. **"Can you send me one research question and one draft you need this week? I'll show you both live."**

Qualified if: 2+ advocates, active litigation or transactional volume, decision-maker on the call or reachable, and they gave you a real question.

## 2. Demo script (20 minutes) — always on the prospect's own matter

| Minute | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Their question typed into Research | "This is the question you sent me. Watch where it searches — Supreme Court, High Court sites, India Code." |
| 2–6 | Answer streaming; Sources panel | "Statute first, then leading cases, then next steps — the way a good senior associate writes." |
| 6–8 | **Citation Guard** | "Every case is checked against the judgments it actually retrieved. Green is verified, amber is a landmark, red means check before you cite. This is why it's safe to put in front of juniors." |
| 8–13 | Drafting → their document type → fill 5 facts → generate | "Missing facts become placeholders — it never invents a date or an amount. Notice it cites BNSS with the CrPC section in brackets." |
| 13–15 | Revise with AI + Download .docx | "Select a paragraph, tell it what to change. Export in A4, Times New Roman 14, 1.5 spacing." |
| 15–17 | Document review → upload a contract/judgment | "Risk table with clause numbers — or a headnote with paragraph references for a judgment." |
| 17–18 | Legal tools | "IPC 420 → BNS 318(4). Limitation for a s.138 complaint. Free and unlimited." |
| 18–20 | Close | "Let's put your whole team on a 14-day trial today. I'll check in on day 3 and we'll review results on day 10." |

**Demo rules:** never demo with the internet flaky (have a recorded backup); never claim 100% accuracy; if an answer is imperfect, say so and show how the advocate would catch it — honesty wins lawyers.

## 3. Trial management

- Day 0: invite every advocate in the firm (Settings → Team). Share the 5-minute "first week" guide from `08-customer-onboarding-and-success.md`.
- Day 3: call the champion. Check usage (Settings → Usage). If fewer than 10 actions, book a 20-minute working session with their juniors.
- Day 10: review call — "What did it save you? What did it get wrong?" Then send the proposal.
- Day 14: trial ends; unpaid firms get a 7-day extension only if a decision meeting is booked.

## 4. Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "AI makes up cases." | "Generic chatbots do. That's exactly why we built Citation Guard — every citation is checked against what was actually retrieved, and anything unverified is flagged red. You still read the judgment before you cite it, as you would with a junior's research." |
| "Client confidentiality." | "Your data is never used to train AI, each firm is isolated, uploads auto-delete after 30 days, and there's a full audit log. We sign a data processing agreement. Enterprise can have a dedicated deployment." |
| "We already pay for SCC / Manupatra." | "Keep it. We're not a database — we're the associate who reads it for you and drafts. Most of our customers use both." |
| "My juniors will stop learning." | "Every answer shows the statute and the reasoning, and every draft lists what to verify. It's closer to a senior sitting next to them than a shortcut." |
| "Too expensive." | "What does an hour of your time bill at? If it saves each advocate two hours a month, it has paid for itself. And the founding-firm price locks in 30% off." |
| "We'll wait until it's more mature." | "The firms using it now are building their precedent library and habits while you wait. Try it for 14 days for free — you lose nothing." |
| "Can it draft in my High Court's format?" | "It knows the standard formats, and on Chambers you can upload your own precedents so it follows your house style exactly." |
| "Is it allowed under Bar Council rules?" | "Using software to assist your work is no different from using a database or a word processor. What the rules require — confidentiality, competence, no solicitation — our product is designed around. You remain responsible for what you file, as always." |

## 5. Mid-size firm pilot (25–150 lawyers)

- **Paid pilot:** 30 days, one practice group, 10 seats, fixed fee (e.g. ₹75,000), credited against an annual contract if signed within 30 days of pilot end.
- **Success criteria agreed in writing upfront:** e.g. ≥ 70% of pilot users active weekly; research turnaround reduced by ≥ 50% on a sample; partner quality rating ≥ 4/5 on 20 reviewed outputs.
- **Security pack ready:** security FAQ, DPA, sub-processor list, data flow diagram, incident response summary.

## 7. CRM hygiene

Stages: Lead → Discovery done → Demo done → Trial active → Proposal sent → Won / Lost (with reason). Every lost deal gets a reason from this list: price, quality, confidentiality, timing, chose competitor, no decision. Review lost reasons monthly — they are your product roadmap.
