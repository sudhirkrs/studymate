# 05 · Outreach templates

Personalise the first line of every message. Never bulk-blast advocates — the community is small and word travels.

## A. Warm network — personal message (WhatsApp / LinkedIn / email)

> Hi [Name], hope practice is going well. I've been building something for litigators and would really value your view: an AI associate for Indian law that researches with checked citations (it flags any case it can't verify) and drafts bail applications, writs, 138 notices and the like in proper formats, BNSS-ready.
>
> Could I show you on one of your own matters? 20 minutes, your question, no slides. If it's not useful I'll take the feedback and disappear.
