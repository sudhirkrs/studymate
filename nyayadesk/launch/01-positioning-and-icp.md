# 01 · Positioning, ideal customer and competition

## One-line positioning

**NyayaDesk is the AI associate for Indian law firms: research with checked citations, court-ready drafts in Indian formats, and document review — in minutes.**

## The problem, in the customer's words

- "My juniors spend two days on research I need by tomorrow morning."
- "Every bail application starts from an old file and a find-and-replace. Mistakes slip through."
- "Since July 2024 half our templates cite the wrong code."
- "I tried ChatGPT. It gave me a Supreme Court judgment that doesn't exist."
- "Good associates are expensive and they leave."

## What we sell (outcomes, not features)

| Outcome | Proof point to show in the demo |
|---|---|
| Research in minutes, not days | Live question → answer with statute, leading cases and next steps in under two minutes |
| No fake citations | Citation Guard panel: Verified / Landmark / Check with links |
| First drafts in Indian formats | Bail application under s.483 BNSS drafted from five facts, exported to Word |
| New criminal laws handled | IPC 420 → BNS 318(4) instantly; drafts cite BNS with IPC in brackets |
| Contract and judgment review | Upload a client's MSA → risk table with clause numbers |
| Firm control | Matters, shared history, audit log, house-style precedents |

## Ideal customer profile (ICP), in priority order

1. **Litigation chambers and boutique firms, 3–25 advocates**, in Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata, Chandigarh, Ahmedabad and Lucknow. Practice: criminal, commercial litigation, arbitration, cheque bounce, consumer, service, property. Buying decision: the senior/managing partner, in one or two meetings. **Best first market.**
2. **Mid-size full-service firms, 25–150 lawyers.** Longer cycle (knowledge-management partner, IT, sometimes a security questionnaire). Higher contract value. Start pilots with one practice group.
3. **In-house legal teams** at companies with 3–20 lawyers (NBFCs, real estate, manufacturing, startups): high contract and notice volume, budget exists. Sold on contract review, notices and research.
4. **Solo advocates** — high volume, low price, self-serve through the website trial. Do not spend sales time here; let the product sell.

Disqualify early: firms that want the AI to give advice directly to their clients; firms unwilling to have any data leave their own servers (route to Enterprise/self-hosted only if the deal size justifies it).

## Competitive landscape

Speak about competitors factually and respectfully; advocates talk to each other.

| Alternative | What it is good at | Where NyayaDesk wins |
|---|---|---|
| SCC Online, Manupatra (and similar subscription databases) | Authoritative reported judgments, citators, headnotes | We complement them: plain-English answers, drafting and review. Position as "use both" — never "cancel your SCC". |
| Indian Kanoon (free) | Free search of judgments | We read and synthesise; we draft; we check citations. |
| General chatbots (ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.) | Cheap, general-purpose | Indian formats, BNS/BNSS awareness, primary-source search, Citation Guard, firm workspace, confidentiality commitments, GST billing. |
| Global legal AI (built for US/UK firms) | Enterprise features | Priced for India, Indian law and formats, Indian billing. |
| Indian legal-AI startups | Varies | Compete on output quality in the demo — bring a real question from the prospect's own practice. |
| Juniors and interns | Judgement, court presence | We make them 3–5x more productive; we don't replace them. |

## Messaging do's and don'ts

- **Do** say "assists advocates", "first draft", "verify before filing".
- **Don't** say "replaces lawyers", "100% accurate", "guaranteed", or "legal advice".
- **Don't** market to the public or offer to connect people with advocates — that runs into the Bar Council of India's rules against solicitation by advocates and makes firms nervous about using us.
- **Do** keep Citation Guard at the centre of every conversation. It is the reason a cautious senior partner will say yes.
