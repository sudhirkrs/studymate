# 08 · Customer onboarding and success

The goal of onboarding: **every advocate in the firm gets one real win in their first week.** Firms that reach 10 actions per seat in week one convert and renew; firms that don't, churn.

## Day 0 — Welcome (automated email + champion call)

> Welcome to NyayaDesk, [Name]. Three things to try this week, each takes under five minutes:
> 1. **Research:** ask the question you'd otherwise give a junior for two days. Look at the Citation Guard panel.
> 2. **Draft:** pick your most common document (bail, 138 notice, reply notice, NDA), fill the facts you know, generate, and download the Word file.
> 3. **Invite your team:** Settings → Team → Invite. The trial covers up to 5 people.
>
> I'll call on [day 3] to see how it's going. — [CSM name, phone]

## First-week guide for advocates (one page — share as PDF)

1. **Ask like you'd brief a junior.** Include the forum, the stage and the key facts. "Sessions Court, Pune; accused in custody 45 days; chargesheet filed; s.318(4) BNS; co-accused on bail" gets a far better answer than "bail for cheating".
2. **Use Research memo** for anything going into a filing; Quick answer for everything else.
3. **Read the red flags.** Anything marked "Check" must be verified before you rely on it.
4. **Pick a matter** in the dropdown — the AI then knows the client, forum and your notes.
5. **Follow-up questions** build on the previous answer: "Now distinguish the contrary Bombay High Court view."
6. **In drafts**, select a paragraph and tell it what to change. Everything is versioned.
7. **Legal tools** (converter, limitation) are free and unlimited — use them daily.

## Onboarding session (Chambers 5+ seats and all Firm plans) — 45 minutes

| Minutes | Agenda |
|---|---|
| 0–10 | Why and how: Citation Guard, confidentiality, the firm's AI-use policy |
| 10–25 | Hands-on: each advocate runs one research question from their own matter |
| 25–35 | Drafting: the firm's three most common documents; set up one firm precedent template |
| 35–45 | Matters, sharing, usage; Q&A |

## Health score (review weekly)

| Signal | Healthy | At risk |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly active advocates / seats | ≥ 60% | < 30% |
| Actions per seat per week | ≥ 5 | < 2 |
| Drafts exported to Word | Growing | Zero after week 2 |
| Firm templates added | ≥ 1 by day 30 | None |
| Support tickets about quality | Answered < 1 day | Unanswered, repeated |

At-risk firms get a call from the founder or CSM within 48 hours, with a working session offer.

## Support

- Channels: email (`SUPPORT_EMAIL`), WhatsApp business number for paying firms, in-app contact.
- Targets: first response < 4 business hours (Chambers), < 1 business hour (Firm/Enterprise). Business hours 9:30–19:30 IST, Monday to Saturday — courts work Saturdays.
- **Quality reports are gold:** every "the AI got this wrong" report is logged with the question and answer, fixed in prompts/templates where possible, and the reporter is told what changed.

## Renewal and expansion

- Day 60: share a usage report with the partner (actions, hours saved estimate = research actions × 2 h + drafts × 1 h).
- Day 75: propose annual (2 months free) with the founding-firm rate locked.
- Expansion triggers: pooled actions > 80% used two months running → propose more seats; new practice group → pilot.
