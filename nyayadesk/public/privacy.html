This policy explains how [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("NyayaDesk") processes personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the rules made under it.

1. Our two roles

  • Account data (names, emails, bar enrolment numbers, billing details of subscribing firms and users): NyayaDesk is the Data Fiduciary.
  • Customer Data (prompts, uploaded documents, matter details, drafts — which may contain personal data of the firm's clients and third parties): the subscribing firm is the Data Fiduciary and NyayaDesk processes it as a Data Processor on the firm's instructions under our Data Processing Agreement.

2. What we collect and why

  • Account and contact details — to create and secure accounts and provide support.
  • Billing details and GSTIN — to process payments (through Razorpay; we do not store card or UPI credentials) and issue tax invoices.
  • Usage and security logs (IP address, sign-ins, actions) — to meter plans, prevent abuse and keep an audit trail for the firm.
  • Customer Data — solely to provide the Service to the firm that submitted it.
  • Demo requests from our website — to contact you about the demo you asked for.

3. No training on your data

We do not use Customer Data to train artificial intelligence models, and our AI model provider processes requests under commercial terms that prohibit training on them.

4. Processors and cross-border transfer

We use the following processors: [HOSTING PROVIDER, REGION] for hosting; Anthropic PBC for AI model processing; Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd. for payments; [EMAIL PROVIDER] for transactional email. Some processing by our AI provider may take place outside India, in countries not restricted by the Central Government under s.16 of the DPDP Act. Enterprise customers may request deployment options that keep processing in specific regions.

5. Retention

  • Uploaded documents: deleted automatically [30] days after upload (configurable for Enterprise).
  • Research, drafts and matter data: kept for the subscription term so the firm can use its history, and deleted within 30 days of account closure.
  • Invoices and billing records: kept for the period required by GST and tax law.
  • Security logs: [12] months.

6. Security

Encryption in transit (TLS), hashed passwords, per-firm data isolation, role-based access, audit logging, and restricted production access for our staff.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction or erasure of your personal data, nominate another person to exercise your rights, and seek grievance redressal. Clients of our customer firms should direct requests to the firm concerned; we will assist the firm. If you are not satisfied with our response you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Personal data breach

We will notify affected customers and the Data Protection Board of a personal data breach as required by the DPDP Act and its rules, and help customer firms meet their own notification obligations.

9. Children

The Service is for legal professionals and is not directed at children.
