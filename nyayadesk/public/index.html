When can a High Court quash proceedings in a non-compoundable offence on the basis of settlement?

Short answer. The High Court may quash under s.528 BNSS (formerly s.482 CrPC) where the dispute is predominantly civil or commercial in character, the parties have genuinely settled, and continuing the prosecution would be oppressive — but not for heinous offences or offences against society such as murder, rape or dacoity…

Key authorities. Gian Singh v. State of Punjab; Narinder Singh v. State of Punjab; Parbatbhai Aahir v. State of Gujarat…

Citation Guard: because a fake citation can end a career

Generic AI chatbots invent case law. Courts have already rebuked lawyers for filing AI-generated citations that do not exist. NyayaDesk is built to stop that.

  • Every case name and citation in an answer is extracted automatically.
  • Each one is matched against the judgments actually retrieved for that answer, and marked Verified, Landmark or Check.
  • "Check" items are flagged in red with a link to verify before you rely on them.
  • Drafts use [ADD AUTHORITY] instead of guessing when the AI is not sure.

Questions advocates ask us

Will it make up case law?

Generic chatbots can. NyayaDesk searches Indian primary sources first, is instructed never to invent authority, and runs Citation Guard over every answer so anything not found in the retrieved sources is flagged for you to check. You should still read the judgments you cite — as you would with a junior's research.

Does it know the new criminal laws?

Yes. It applies the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam to matters after 1 July 2024, gives the old IPC/CrPC/Evidence Act section in brackets, and flags where transitional provisions matter.

Does it cover the new Labour Codes?

Yes. Answers and drafts apply the Code on Wages, the Industrial Relations Code, the Code on Social Security and the OSH Code, cite the old Act's section in brackets, and flag where State rules or State Shops and Establishments law decide the point. The converter maps all 29 repealed labour Acts to the Codes, and document review includes a Labour Codes compliance audit for appointment letters, HR policies and contractor agreements.

Is my clients' information safe?

Your data is used only to answer your requests and is never used to train AI models. Each firm's workspace is isolated, uploads are deleted automatically after the retention period, and every access is logged. We sign a data processing agreement with every firm.

Can it draft in my court's format?

It knows the standard formats for District Courts, High Courts, the Supreme Court and major tribunals. On the Chambers plan and above you can add your own precedents so drafts follow your house style exactly. Drafts export to Word in A4, Times New Roman 14, 1.5 spacing.

Does it replace SCC Online or Manupatra?

No — use it alongside them. NyayaDesk gets you to the answer and the leading authorities fast; your subscription database is where you pull the certified report for filing.

How does billing work?

Monthly or annual subscription through Razorpay — UPI Autopay, cards or e-mandate. A GST tax invoice is generated automatically every cycle; add your GSTIN to claim input tax credit. Cancel any time.
